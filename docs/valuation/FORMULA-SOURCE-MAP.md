## Evidence Strength

- `PRIMARY`: original paper, standard, dataset, or official methodology.
- `SECONDARY`: credible explanation or synthesis tied to primary evidence.
- `GENERATED`: NotebookLM, agent, or other model-produced synthesis/prototype.
- `PRODUCT-POLICY`: owner-selected behavior not dictated by research.

## Source Intake Rules

- Use stable inventory asset IDs.
- One row represents one testable mathematical or policy claim.
- Split compound claims.
- Quote sparingly and record exact source location.
- Generated material must trace to underlying evidence where possible.
- Conflicts remain explicit until an APPROVED decision resolves them.
- No claim advances beyond PROPOSED without units, ranges, assumptions, and boundary behavior.
